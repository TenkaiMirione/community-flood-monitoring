3D Printing Seminar

a seminar on 3D printing, also known as additive manufacturing. It explains how additive manufacturing differs from traditional subtractive methods, outlines the main 3D printing technologies (SCA, SCS, FDM), and highlights applications in architecture, medicine, education, and business. The reflection also examines the potential impact of 3D printing on traditional industries and emphasizes its role in fostering creativity, innovation, and the ability to turn digital designs into tangible objects.

Freshie Salubong

The Freshie Salubong event, held on August 22, 2025, featured fun and interactive games designed to welcome new students. Activities included Charades, Sack Race, and the Calamansi Relay, providing opportunities for teamwork, laughter, and bonding among participants.
